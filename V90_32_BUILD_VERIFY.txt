- Physical Google reroute transition 5→0→3→1 is preserved through a 3-second same-source grace; ordinary route-end state 0 is unchanged.

- Fresh CarPlay currentRoad biases Improved + Philly OSM/GIS road selection only.

- Upcoming/afterRoad is used only as a completed-turn geometry tie-breaker (<=20 m, <=25 deg, <=180 m maneuver distance).
- N/S/E/W and common street suffix normalization added for provider/OSM road-name matching.
- Posted speed values remain exclusively OSM/Philadelphia GIS derived.
