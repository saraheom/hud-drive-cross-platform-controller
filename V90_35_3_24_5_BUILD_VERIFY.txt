MainVideo recovery contract:
1. One decoder-stall episode may request one TCP recent-anchor reseed.
2. If that bootstrap fails before any decoded frame, no second recovery reconnect is issued.
3. The current TCP/H.264 stream is preserved and VideoToolbox waits for the next genuinely live IDR.
4. A successfully decoded frame resets the one-reseed budget.
5. Actual TCP failure/source silence retains the normal transport reconnect path.
