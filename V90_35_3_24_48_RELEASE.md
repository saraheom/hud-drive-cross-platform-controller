# HUD Controller v90.35.3.24.48 — Production UI simplification and bounded reliability

Base: v24.47 complete iOS 26 repository; U2W v8.40 remains unchanged.

## Custom Map Mode: simplified main-tab card
- Keep the live preview, three saved design presets, Wi-Fi SSID/password, **Save adapter Wi-Fi & retry Map Mode**, persistent **Automatic Map Mode**, a concise stream-state label, **Prevent Auto-Lock during Map Mode**, and the expandable full-canvas layout designer.
- Remove the parked **Test stock HUD message**, manual **Collect Live Map Diagnostic ZIP**, FPS selector, inline decoder/background telemetry, and long developer explanations from the everyday controls. Underlying log/diagnostic capture functions remain in source for future troubleshooting.
- Production JPEG rendering now uses the highest existing validated FPS setting, **15 FPS**, ignoring a previously saved lower FPS preference. This is the target render rate, **not** a promise that actual measured throughput exceeds the 11–13 FPS observed in drive logs; the U2W hardware/relay and decoder still constrain throughput.
- Remove the **BLEDIM PRODUCTION ANIMATION** UI section from Ambient. The underlying Already-On Minimal and synchronized light animation behavior stays unchanged.

## HUD Map Mode startup resilience
- Treat an unavailable/timeout relay-status CGI reply as **indeterminate**, not proof that the HUD MJPEG client disconnected. Wait for up to five consecutive indeterminate probes before treating it as an outage.
- Require three confirmed negative viewer-status checks before entering the existing bounded recovery, rather than reacting to two negative checks.
- If an automatic viewer rejoin fails due to transient relay unavailability or incomplete rejoin, retry **once** with a bounded 6-second delay while automatic Map Mode remains desired and BLE remains connected. Preserve the existing two-attempt per-session cap and stock-mode fallback if the viewer cannot recover.
- The previous startup mode/connection ordering, saved adapter credentials and U2W firmware remain unchanged. The initial CarPlay head-unit “Connecting…” failure is upstream and cannot be proven fixed by these changes.

## Video continuity for Google Maps and Apple Maps
- After a real mid-drive VideoToolbox `-8969` failure and 8 seconds of continued incoming bytes without a decodable IDR, attempt a passive retained-history bootstrap; if still not recovered after 25 seconds, allow **one more** read-only attempt (maximum two for the episode). Existing decoder, stream and adapter remain intact outside the bounded attempt.
- Never trigger CarPlay screen switches or unverified source/keyframe injection. A new source IDR can still be necessary; source-side video failures cannot be guaranteed fixed by the app alone.
- Do not treat reverse-camera display changes at arrival as a mid-drive defect.

## Current + Nav: earlier evidence on initially untagged road segments
- Retain offline Philadelphia OSM, exact CarPlay street-name/GPS-geometry checks, the v24.47 30-second missing-geometry bridge and same-road display-only continuity.
- Extend connected **same-named** OSM segment inspection to up to 30 hops and 850 meters from the current GPS location, requiring endpoint topological continuity within 20 meters, no incompatible motorway/trunk class transitions and one unanimous explicit OSM speed tag. Conflicting tagged speeds yield **unknown**, not a guessed limit.
- Same-road inferred limits remain display-only with overspeed warnings disabled. Explicit current-road speed tags always win. No Philadelphia municipal GIS or neighborhood default added.
