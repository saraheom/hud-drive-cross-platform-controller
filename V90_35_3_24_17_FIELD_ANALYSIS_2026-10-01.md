## Cross-layer findings

### Adapter / AppleCarPlay source

The source did not die at MLK Drive. The passive observer remained `LIVE`, recorded continued valid access units, source-generation changes, codec refreshes, and explicit `frame_num` discontinuities. Separate end-of-drive v8.11 status showed the exporter still active with hundreds of MiB mirrored and dozens of IDRs.

### iPhone raw transport

TCP/15332 continued delivering bytes after visible decode output stopped. The final v24.16 state recorded about 147.5 MB received during its active preview window. The iPhone Annex-B sanitizer accepted thousands of VCL candidates while also rejecting a very large number of false/contaminated candidates from the raw mirror.

### VideoToolbox

The decisive failure is downstream of transport: VideoToolbox output stopped while raw bytes continued. Final v24.16 state contained 4,111 output-callback failures with `outputStatus=-8969`.

The old policy allowed three `-8969` failures to merely arm a future-IDR rebuild. That is insufficient after a missing-reference/source-generation boundary: the session can continue receiving dependent pictures that are syntactically valid but impossible to decode because their reference frame is absent.

## Root cause addressed by v24.17

The iPhone sanitizer already decoded H.264 `frame_num`, but used it only as part of a multi-slice identity key. It did not enforce reference-picture continuity. v24.17 turns that parsed value into a reference-chain safety gate and pairs it with bounded `-8969` escalation.

This explains the apparent OEM-screen/reverse-camera correlation: a display transition can coincide with a clean new codec/reference epoch, which makes the decoder recover. The backup camera itself is not the repair mechanism.
