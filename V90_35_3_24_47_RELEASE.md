# HUD Controller v90.35.3.24.47 — MLK same-road geometry-gap continuity

## Why v24.46 could still hide the 25 mph sign

The October 29 afternoon drive shows a direct OSM `ACCEPT` for Martin Luther King Junior Drive at 25 mph at 22:17:14Z, followed by successive `REJECT` results when the offline index had no eligible MLK segment at 22:17:15–30Z. The existing 12-second/150-meter geometry-gap hold expired, and a visible sign was cleared at 22:17:23Z. A direct OSM MLK 25 mph match returned at 22:17:31Z. The v24.46 long continuity state only applied when the matcher found a *geometrically accepted untagged segment*, so it did not cover this missing-geometry/offline-tile seam.

## v24.47 changes — Current + Nav only

1. Preserve the existing 12-minute/6-km independent **geometry-verified untagged-road** continuity. When the app directly matches an explicitly tagged road and CarPlay still identifies the same road, the prior tagged limit can remain visible through matching untagged segments. No legal limit is inferred from the street name alone.
2. Introduce **bounded missing-geometry continuity** (`CurrentNavSameRoadContinuity.bridgeUnmappedGap`): if the last explicitly tagged limit has a confirmed road identity and recent GPS geometry match, and the OSM tile momentarily lacks any eligible geometry, the displayed value can bridge **up to 30 seconds / 550 meters from the LAST geometry-verified position**. The app requires **fresh CarPlay current-street metadata**, an unchanged normalized street identity, no navigation turn/reroute transition, no plausible nearby different road, and no nearby conflicting tagged same-road speed. A successful bridge **does not advance or refresh** the last geometry-confirmed position/time; repeated calls cannot extend the grace interval.
3. A carried sign is **display only**; it never enables overspeed warnings. The log prints `CONTINUITY_GAP_DISPLAY_ONLY ... warn=0`; matching confirmed untagged segments continues to print `CONTINUITY_DISPLAY_ONLY ... warn=0`. An explicit newly matched posted speed overrides prior carried speed immediately.
4. Keep the previous 12-second short hold independently and keep CarPlay road-change invalidation. A long gap, nonmatching road, stale CarPlay, or contradictory road/speed will end continuity rather than displaying an invented limit.
5. Update diagnostic app version to `v90.35.3.24.47`.
