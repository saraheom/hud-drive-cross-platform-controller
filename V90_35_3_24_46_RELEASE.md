# HUD Controller v90.35.3.24.46 — same-road explicit-speed continuity

## Motivation

The October 29 MLK Drive logs confirm the app obtained a directly tagged 25 mph limit, continued matching `Martin Luther King Junior Drive` within a few meters, and subsequently returned `UNKNOWN` on correctly named untagged geometry when its previous 12-second hold expired. The offline OSM road snapshot was loaded (15,236 segments; 3,270 with supported speed tags). No default 25 mph value is inferred.

## Changes (Current + Nav only)

- Introduce **CurrentNavSameRoadContinuity.swift**, a small pure-Foundation, independently tested state machine. An explicitly tagged and geometrically accepted OSM limit creates a *street-specific anchor*. Subsequent untagged segments on the **same CarPlay current street**, when independently accepted by the existing GPS-to-road geometry and heading gates, can show that value without momentarily blanking the speed-limit sign.
- A carried limit never gains warning eligibility and never refreshes its original explicit verification timestamp. Continuity expires after **12 minutes or 6,000 meters of cumulative confirmed GPS movement** since the latest directly matched explicit OSM speed, whichever occurs first. A sudden GPS jump is rejected. This bounds propagation in long untagged corridors, but does NOT establish that the actual posted limit never changes; obey roadside signs.
- When the current street changes (including to an expressway), the anchor is revoked. A mismatched or geometrically ineligible road cannot inherit the limit. A different directly tagged speed on the same road overrides immediately. Nearby eligible competing tagged same-road speed evidence blocks carrying or connected-way inference when incompatible.
- The existing 12-second / 150-meter hold remains as a separate, short **missing-GPS-geometry** grace period; prolonged road-name-only matching cannot extend the new continuity.
- **Named-road OSM audits still run during a carried sign**, so the app can acquire a missing explicit tag even while the HUD shows a continuity value. Diagnostic logs include `CONTINUITY_DISPLAY_ONLY` with identity, speed, original age, cumulative travel, and `warn=0`.
- Diagnostic app identifier updated to `v90.35.3.24.46`.
